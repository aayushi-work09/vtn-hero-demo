Count the Stars

The night the lights went out, Mira counted stars from the rooftop, one for every mistake she had made that year.

“You are late,” said the stranger beside her. She had not heard him climb up. His voice was calm, as if he had been waiting for years.

“For what?” she asked. He smiled and pointed at the sky, where one star was moving too fast to be a star at all.

She should have been afraid. Instead she felt something she had not felt in a long time: the sense that her story was about to begin.
